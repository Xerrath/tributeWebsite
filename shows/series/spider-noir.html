Overview

Spider-Noir is an American superhero television series developed by Oren Uziel for MGM+ and Prime Video. Based on Marvel Comics featuring the character Spider-Man Noir, the series follows an aging private investigator and superhero in 1930s New York City grappling with his past. Uziel and Steve Lightfoot serve as the series showrunners. Produced by Sony Pictures Television in association with Amazon MGM Studios, Spider-Noir is set in an alternate universe within the Sony's Spider-Man Universe (SSU) franchise.

Nicolas Cage stars as Ben Reilly / The Spider in his first lead role in a television series, with Lamorne Morris, Li Jun Li, Karen Rodriguez, Abraham Popoola, Jack Huston, and Brendan Gleeson also starring. Series development and Uziel's involvement were revealed in February 2023; Lightfoot was hired that December. Cage's casting was confirmed in May 2024, when the series was ordered and titled Noir before it was retitled Spider-Noir that July. Filming took place in Los Angeles from August 2024 to March 2025.

Spider-Noir was released in its entirety on May 25, 2026, on MGM+ in the United States, in color, and consists of eight episodes. The series was later released globally on Prime Video on May 27, in two versions stylized as Authentic Black and White and True-Hue Full Color. It received positive reviews from critics, with Cage's performance receiving particular praise. The series received 11 Primetime Emmy Award nominations, winning five. In September 2026, the series was canceled after one season.
